what is the Tong Shu, exactly?
The Tong Shu (通書) is the 2,500-year-old Chinese almanac, the system most Chinese families consult for wedding dates, opening a business, signing contracts, & starting anything that matters. flowtongshu runs the same readings, instantly, in plain English.
do I need to know Chinese astrology?
Not at all. Every reading is in plain English. Tap any term (pillar, branch, day officer) for a one-line explanation. The system does the work; you just read your day.
how does flowtongshu actually pick a date?
Four steps, every time. It reads your chart, clears every day that clashes with it, ranks what's left by the 12 Day Officers & star quality for your purpose, then helps you lock the best hour, & shows you the reasoning behind each.
how does the 3-day free trial work?
Pick any paid plan & get full access for 3 days. Cancel anytime before day 3 to avoid charges. If you don't continue, your account stays on Free with the daily reading intact.
can a real master check my date?
Yes. For high-stakes events you can request a Master-Verified review on top of your engine results, with a written rationale you keep & can share.
is my birth data private?
Always. Birth profiles are encrypted, never sold, never shared, never used for advertising. What's yours stays yours.
how is this different from ChatGPT or free apps?
They don't run the actual Bazi math. They generate plausible-sounding text. flowtongshu uses a classical engine built on solar terms, the 24-hour 時辰 system, & your real four-pillar chart, & shows its reasoning for every date.
