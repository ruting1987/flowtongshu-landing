Questions

Things people ask

Short answers to what comes up most.

What is the Tong Shu, exactly?
The Tong Shu (通書) is the 2,500-year-old Chinese almanac — the source most Chinese families consult for choosing wedding dates, opening businesses, signing contracts, or starting anything that matters. Flow Tong Shu computes the same readings, instantly, in plain English.
Do I need to know Chinese astrology?
Not at all. Every reading is in plain English. Tap any term — pillar, branch, day officer — for a one-line explanation. The classical wisdom does the work; you just read your day.
How does the 3-day free trial work?
Pick any paid plan, get full access for 3 days. No credit card required. If you don't continue, your account stays — just on the Free tier with the daily reading.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Two clicks from your account, anytime. You'll keep paid access until the end of the billing period — no questions, no friction.
Is my birth data private?
Always. Your birth profile is encrypted, never sold, never shared, never used for advertising. We follow the same data principles a temple would: what's yours stays yours.
What's the difference between the plans?
Free gives you the daily reading. Lite adds the monthly view and journal. Pro unlocks the date selector — the most-used feature for planning real events. Master extends your range to ±12 months. Business is for teams that plan together.
Does it work in any time zone?
Yes. Flow Tong Shu calculates pillars and day officers based on solar terms in your local time zone, so the reading is always correct for where you are.
